Ask where to go, find what you need and get out. A voice guide for ARC Raiders.

Ask. Find. Get out.

ArcRelay is a voice guide for ARC Raiders. Ask out loud and it answers out loud, so your eyes stay on the screen and your hands stay on the pad. No alt-tabbing, no scrolling a guide mid-raid.

Say "Hey Relay", or tap, and ask like you would ask a teammate:

• "Get me out of here." The nearest extraction from where you are, with the direction and distance.
• "Not that one, it's closed." It rules that exit out for the rest of the raid and gives you the next best.
• "What's next on Home to Roost?" Quest steps one at a time, and it remembers where you got to.
• "Where can I find Dead Flowers?" The best place to look, and an honest answer when an item has no fixed spot.
• "Anything south of me?" Exits and places in that direction.

Tell it where you are by name ("I'm at the Village"), by side of the map ("south-east"), or tap the map and confirm. Every answer comes with a map showing you and where to go.

Built to run on your phone
• Answers are worked out on the phone from game data built into the app.
• A natural offline voice, downloaded once (about 130 MB). After that, no internet needed for speaking.
• Speech recognition runs on the phone where Android supports it.
• "Hey Relay" only listens for those two words, only while the app is open on screen. You can turn it off.
• Short answers, said once. After an answer it listens once more for a follow-up, then goes quiet.

Free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Early access
ArcRelay currently covers Blue Gate. More maps will follow. If it gets something wrong, let us know and we will fix it.
