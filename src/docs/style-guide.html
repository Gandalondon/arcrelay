ArcRelay style guide

How ArcRelay looks, sounds and behaves, taken from the app as built. Read it before adding a screen, a map or a spoken line. Reuse what is here; if something is missing, flag it under Gaps rather than inventing a new pattern.

The appwww/index.html is the whole app (one page, Capacitor wraps it for Android). Native parts: android/app/src/main/java/app/spottr/companion/. GitHub builds an APK on every push (v1.0.N), which Obtainium installs.
The sitesite/index.html (holding page, getarcrelay.app) and site/beta/ (the full site, unlisted).
Source of truthThe live build wins over this guide and older briefs. When they disagree, fix the guide.
House rulesUK English. No em dashes. No widows: headings use text-wrap: balance, body text text-wrap: pretty. Three colours. One typeface. Material Symbols Rounded icons only. £0 to run: no paid services, keys or fonts.
This filesrc/docs/style-guide.html, built by tools/style-guide/make.py. Opens straight in a browser, offline.

Foundations

Dark, calm and high contrast, for a phone propped beside a TV in a dim room. Amber means "you can act on this". No greys, no borders on app surfaces: depth comes from the charcoal and panel pair.

Answer headlines with a map are one fixed size, 48 px, three lines at most, in a box that is always three lines tall (147 px), so the map sits in the same place on every answer. The context line keeps its one line too, even when empty. Write to fit: aim for 40 characters. Safety net only: a line that still won't fit steps down 44 → 40 → 36 → 32 inside the same box (fitInstr()); if that happens on a 412 px screen, shorten the line.

Voice states

The screen must always tell the truth about the microphone. If the halo is breathing, Relay is hearing you; if not, it isn't.

StandbyHome or the last answer, mic idle. "Hey Relay" listens for just those two words while Relay is open on screen (offline keyword spotter, nothing transcribed). Home's context line: "Say “Hey Relay”, or tap."
ListeningAfter the wake phrase or a tap. Halo and waveform. Speech recognition on the phone where Android supports it.
SpeakingOutlined mic with waveform. The microphone is off, so Relay never hears itself.
Follow-upAfter an answer, one more listen of about 8 seconds. Nothing heard: straight back to standby. Never loops.
Off screenIn the background or in Settings, nothing listens.

Motion

Few, quick, purposeful. Everything respects reduced motion.

StartAndroid reports exactly where it drew the icon; Relay draws the same lines there, then they drop out of the circle (masked at the top, open below), outer line first, 520 ms each, 70 ms apart, easing in like gravity. Relay fades in once they're gone. Never animate over text.
Halo1.6 s breathe while listening.
State changesMic colour 0.15 s. Screen fade 0.2 s.
Site logoLines draw in (440 ms, 70 ms apart), then the wordmark wipes in left to right. Home page only, once per visit; a refresh replays it.

Spoken copy

Relay is a calm teammate on comms. It's heard, not read, often mid-fight.

Do

  • Lead with the destination, then direction, then distance: "Cliffside Airshaft, about 150 metres south-east."
  • Keep it short when time is short: "All four airshafts marked. Where are you?"
  • Vary openers ("Try", "Head for", "Next best is").
  • Say what you don't know: "No fixed spot. Try the Village."
  • Remember the raid: exits ruled out stay ruled out.
  • Give full help once, then a short reply.

Don't

  • Ask what the player can see ("Can you see a hatch?").
  • Read out tags or categories ("shops").
  • Repeat "Based on your current position".
  • Claim live exit status, or a route around obstacles.
  • Use em dashes, American spelling or "'d" contractions in written copy.

Accessibility

ContrastCream on charcoal 13.7:1. Cream on panel 12.1:1. Ink on amber 9.6:1. Amber on charcoal 9.0:1. Amber on panel 7.9:1. Cream at 75% on charcoal 8.3:1. All pass WCAG AA and AAA for body text.
Not colour aloneMap markers differ in shape as well as colour (solid, dashed ring, hollow ring, amber). Selected rows also change their meta text ("Selected").
FocusEvery control shows a visible ring: amber on charcoal, cream on amber.
Screen readersThe map has a text label of what it shows. Icon buttons have labels. Voice state is announced in a live region ("Listening", "Speaking").
MotionReduced motion skips the start animation and the halo.
Targets48 px wherever possible (see Gaps for the map buttons).

Gaps to raise, not fix silently

  • Name. The app on the phone is called "Relay" (capacitor.config.json, page title); the store listing and site say ArcRelay. Decide before Play.
  • Secondary text. Cream at 75% opacity is used for notes and meta. It's effectively a grey. Keep as the one exception, or drop it.
  • Token names. CSS calls charcoal --navy and cream --bone, and --grey is cream. Legacy names; rename together when convenient.
  • Map buttons are 44 px, under Android's 48 dp guideline. Fine in testing; raise if misses happen.
  • Map credit wording differs: app "Maps by MetaForge", site mockups "Map: MetaForge".
  • Leftover styles for a cream text input (.gkey input) from the removed Gemini key screen.
  • No light theme. Intentional for now (dark room, beside a TV).
  • Playable area. Taps outside Blue Gate's playable area are accepted until MetaForge publishes a boundary.

Checklist before shipping a change

  • Only charcoal, panel, cream, amber and ink. Amber only for things you can act on, or the destination.
  • Barlow, sizes from the scale. Headline with a map: 48 px, three lines max.
  • Material Symbols Rounded, from the icon list.
  • Mic in the same place, halo only when the mic is on.
  • No widows; no em dashes; UK spelling.
  • Map credit visible; no invented routes.
  • Checked at 360, 412 and 1280 px wide; site mockups still match the app.
  • Spoken line read aloud: under about eight words when urgent.
  • Tests pass (node tests/run.js).
  • This guide updated if a pattern changed.
